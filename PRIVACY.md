# Skriti privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Skriti is a goal tracker that works entirely on your phone.

## What Skriti stores
Your goals, the days you marked done or missed, notes you type (why a goal matters, what happened when you missed), settings, and the apps you choose to block or watch. All of it is kept in a database inside the app on your phone.

## What leaves your phone
Nothing. Skriti has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer cannot see your data.

## The accessibility service
If you switch on Skriti's blocker in Android's Accessibility settings, Skriti learns only the name of the app that is in front and the time. It never reads what is on the screen, what you type, or any content inside an app. It uses this to show a block screen over apps you chose, and to ask "Did you just use this app?" for goals you set to avoid an app. The answer stays on your phone.

## Backups
"Save backup file" writes a file to a place you choose. Skriti does not upload it. Anyone who has the file can read your goals and notes, so keep it somewhere you trust.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling Skriti deletes all of its data. Save a backup first if you want to keep your history.

## Children
Skriti is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is published in this file in the project repository with a new date.
